Frequently asked questions

How do I log a reaction during a walk?

Tap the trigger button (Dog, Person, Bike…), choose the intensity and distance, and save. To log instantly with default values, long-press the button instead. You can also log from the Lock Screen while a walk is running.

The Lock Screen buttons don't appear.

Check iPhone Settings → ReactiDog → Live Activities is on. The buttons appear only while a walk is running.

My walk route isn't on the map.

ReactiDog needs location access "While Using the App". Go to iPhone Settings → ReactiDog → Location. Reactions are still logged without location, just without map pins.

Why don't I see AI summaries?

AI features run on-device and need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with it turned on in Settings. On other iPhones, ReactiDog shows its built-in summaries instead.

Can I use Siri or the Action button?

Yes. Say "Start a walk in ReactiDog" or "Log a dog reaction in ReactiDog". You can also assign "Start walk" to the Action button in iPhone Settings → Action Button → Shortcut.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Open ReactiDog → Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → ReactiDog, or ReactiDog → Settings → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Your data comes along when you restore an iCloud or computer backup. You can also save a copy at any time with Settings → Export my data.

My dog bit someone or the reactions are getting worse.

Please contact a certified force-free trainer or a veterinary behaviourist, and ask your vet to rule out pain or illness. ReactiDog is a training companion, not veterinary advice. Your trainer report (Insights → Trainer report) can help them.
