Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · ReactiDog is made by Hicham Balaich, Morocco.

The short version: ReactiDog has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Everything you log stays on your iPhone. We can't see your data.

What the app stores, on your device only

  • Your dog's profile: name, breed, photo, triggers and goal
  • Your walks: route, duration, distance and notes
  • Reactions and calm moments: time, type, intensity, distance and location
  • Voice-note transcripts you choose to record
  • Training progress and app settings

This data is kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone. It's included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your own backup settings.

Location

ReactiDog asks for location access "While Using the App" and uses it only while a walk is running, to draw your route and pin reactions on the map. You can hide the area around your home in Settings. Location data is never sent to us or anyone else.

Microphone and speech recognition

Used only while you hold the voice-note button. Speech is transcribed on your iPhone. Audio isn't stored or sent anywhere.

AI features

Walk summaries, the weekly coach and voice-note parsing use Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Your data is processed on your iPhone and isn't shared with any third-party AI provider.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to them.

Data we collect

None. We don't collect, sell or share personal data, and the app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Your choices

  • Export everything: Settings → Export my data
  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app

Children

ReactiDog isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and mention the change in the app's release notes.
